    What does a sourced finding look like?

    Bounded finding: Mary Shelley later recalled frequent rain during the summer when Frankenstein began.

    Exact passage: “But it proved a wet, ungenial summer, and incessant rain often confined us for days to the house.”

    Source: Shelley’s 1831 introduction to Frankenstein.

    Limit: This is a retrospective account. It supports the rainy setting; it does not establish weather as the sole cause of the story.

    This illustrates claim, passage and limitation. Use the full prescribed format from your prompt for the file you upload.
