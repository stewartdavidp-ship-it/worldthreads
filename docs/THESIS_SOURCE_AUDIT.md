# WorldThreads thesis stress test: patronage and instruction in Brazil

Reviewed 6 October 2026. Scope: one thesis exercised by the complete-game test, plus an independent agent challenge of its sources. This is a pilot source audit, not scholarly certification.

The useful result is a narrower thesis. The decree supports authorization toward proposed instruction. It cannot establish that the institution was immediately operating, that authorized pensions were paid, or that teaching reached particular students. Retrospective accounts also preserve an unresolved building chronology.

## A defensible working thesis

The 1816 decree authorized patronage toward proposed instruction; it does not establish immediate institutional operation. Retrospective accounts describe private teaching and an 1826 inauguration, but differ about building readiness. Dated teaching, attendance, contractual and payment records are needed to establish implementation and its scale.

## What changed in the player flow

A regression showed that two evidence entries sharing one source escaped the warning when a third distinct source was added. The app now flags each detected group of overlapping sources, including registered observations and imported candidates using the same URL with different passage fragments. It keeps these reminders in the thesis export, presentation stage and filed portfolio snapshot.

Different URLs are not certified as independent. Mirrors, different editions and two authors drawing on the same archive still need a human provenance review. The reminders neither award points nor require the player to invent another source. An openly limited proposal can still be filed as unreviewed preparation.

## Research that would change the argument

1. Locate dated class, enrollment and teaching records. Distinguish private instruction from full institutional operation.
2. Compare pension authorization with payment ledgers and signed contracts. Record missing or inaccessible material without treating absence as proof.
3. Locate the building-use requests and inauguration records. Keep readiness, access, inauguration and teaching as separate claims.
4. Trace each retrospective account’s citations. Shared originals cannot count twice as independent corroboration.
5. Test the rival that patronage supported a developing institution and private teaching before full operation. State what evidence would distinguish it from immediate full institutional operation.
