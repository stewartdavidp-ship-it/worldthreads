## Remaining limits

- Regional proxy dryness does not establish Bengal crop losses, famine or a Tambora–cholera causal link; the latter remains a research gap.
- Colonial documents conflict and encode official viewpoints. Appin’s complete death toll remains uncertain; the proclamation’s sequence does not prove its cause.
- Andean preparation plausibly enabled access. Chronology does not explain victory or prove inevitable independence.
- Mechanisms remain hypotheses. Empty counterexample lists mean research is incomplete. Sumbawa lacks a price series and local relief-effectiveness evidence.
- Inherited records received targeted checks, not a full archival replication. “Verified” in older records is a legacy review label, not certainty.
