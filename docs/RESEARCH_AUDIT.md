## Cross-review corrections and boundaries

- Korean paddy-tax denominators cover six assessed provinces; harvest reporting covers eight. The good rice harvest classification is a counterexample to uniform crop failure, not proof every household avoided hardship.
- Kumaun SPI7-May reconstructs November–May precipitation, not the summer monsoon. It does not measure Bengal crops or prove cholera attribution.
- Jessore's 23 August and Calcutta's 15 September 1817 dates are report dates, not infection onsets. Colonial records describe fear-driven flight and uneven exposure without measuring the transmission effects.
- Bali rice disruption, October 1816 mortality reporting and an unspecified 1817 epidemic are separated. Food-import access and purchasing power matter alongside crop damage; disease identity is unresolved.
- The Tahiti pork vessel arrived on 28 May, not 1 May. Hawaiian gifts were dispatched on 1 May; receipt is not established. NSW flood crop figures are contemporary expectations, not measured final harvest losses.
- Macquarie's proclamation was issued on 4 May; principal weapons/gathering controls were scheduled for 4 June. Transfer of captive children does not establish free consent.
- Brazil's August decree authorized artist pensions toward proposed instruction; it does not prove a school opened in 1816. Mendoza's approximate worker estimate is not a dated census.
- Radama's Mauritius visitors are described as brothers by the dispatch but as nephews elsewhere. Arrival is reported by 12 September, not proven to occur that day. February and October 1817 agreements remain distinct; a slave-export prohibition does not prove enforcement or abolition of domestic slavery.
- Shipboard air-temperature series, coral SST proxies and simulated ocean heat content retain different variables and timescales. Greenland Sea relative warmth and Korean wetness provide concrete contrasts to uniform climate claims.
- Dutch stock surveys, public imports and subsidized-sale authorization are documented. Import timing and distribution limits prevent equating policy intentions with effective relief.
- Swiss potato reductions are potential yields simulated with current cultivars; modeled crop sensitivity alone does not explain the observed malnutrition gradient. Virginia crop forecasts remain forecasts, separate from local temperature/rainfall reports.
