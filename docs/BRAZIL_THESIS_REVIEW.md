Direct access failed in this pass; the operative text was readable through the official search index. Authorization does not establish payment, signed contracts or classroom operation. A printed facsimile was not inspected.

Its displayed primary document is an 1840 honorary appointment. That document is not an 1816 attendance record.

## Consequences for the investigation

Preserve the building-date disagreement rather than choosing or averaging dates. Agreement on institutional operation does not demonstrate that these accounts are independent. Investigate their underlying references.

The retrospective account weakens the inference that a decree proves full operation. It does not complete the pilot’s proposed class-register/payroll test. That archive search remains pending.
