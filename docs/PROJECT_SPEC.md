# WorldThreads — Project Specification v1

## Purpose
WorldThreads models history as interconnected systems rather than a list of isolated events. A year is an interface into the knowledge graph, not the fundamental unit of history.

## Core idea
History is produced by interacting conditions, shocks, people, institutions, natural systems, responses and outcomes. WorldThreads records observations and evidence, connects them into mechanisms and threads, and allows similar causal structures to be compared across places and periods.

## Prototype
1816 is the first prototype year. Research should examine a wider temporal window, initially 1814–1818, so causes and consequences are not artificially cut off at calendar-year boundaries.

## Primary analytical systems
- EARTH — atmosphere, temperature, precipitation, storms, volcanism, oceans, rivers, lakes, drought, floods, snow, glaciers and sea ice.
- BIOSPHERE — crops, vegetation, insects, wildlife, fisheries, livestock, disease ecology and biological proxies.
- PRODUCTION & RESOURCES — agriculture, food, raw materials, energy, transport and productive capacity.
- HUMAN SYSTEMS — population, health, migration, trade, prices, employment, technology, communications and social conditions.
- POWER & CULTURE — politics, institutions, war, diplomacy, religion, ideas, science, art, literature, music and material culture.

## Fundamental evidence unit: Observation
An observation is the smallest stored piece of historical evidence. Examples include a measured temperature, a river height, a grain price, a crop failure report, an insect swarm, fish exports, livestock deaths, migration, a political decision or an artwork. Observations remain separate from interpretations of their relationships.

## Evidence types
- Instrumental measurement
- Contemporary documentary observation
- Administrative/economic record
- Biological proxy
- Geological proxy
- Modern reconstruction
- Scholarly interpretation

## Data quality
Every observation should carry geography, time/date precision, source, evidence type, confidence/uncertainty and review status. Reconstructed values must never silently appear equivalent to contemporary instrumental measurements.

## Analytical role tags
- STATE — pre-existing condition
- PRESSURE — force building within a system
- SHOCK — sudden change
- RESPONSE — action by people or institutions
- OUTCOME — consequence
- RESILIENCE — factor interrupting or reducing an adverse chain
- LAG — elapsed time between connected observations
- MECHANISM — recurring causal process potentially comparable across history
- ADAPTATION — behavioral or institutional change in response to pressure

## Relationship vocabulary
CAUSED_BY, CONTRIBUTED_TO, EXACERBATED_BY, ENABLED, CONSTRAINED, RESPONDED_TO, ACCELERATED, DISRUPTED, COINCIDED_WITH, CONTESTED_CONNECTION, ADAPTATION_TO.

Relationships are claims and require evidence. Correlation or temporal proximity must not automatically be stored as causation.

## Global coverage rule
Apply the same depth of inquiry to each major world region. Do not force equal numbers of records. Absence of accessible evidence must be represented as a research gap rather than absence of history.

## Signature experiences
- YEAR — explore a cross-section such as 1816
- TRACE CAUSES — traverse evidence-supported connections backward
- TRACE CONSEQUENCES — traverse connections forward
- FIND SIMILAR THREADS — identify structurally comparable mechanisms elsewhere in history
- WHY DIFFERENT? — compare similar pressures that produced different outcomes and identify possible resilience factors
- WHAT HAPPENED NEXT? — display historical outcome distributions without presenting them as deterministic predictions

## Working slogan
History isn't a collection of events. It's the threads that connect them.

## Year-by-year community expansion

Build a reviewed annual foundation, invite local research, verify and incorporate contributions, then open another deliberately selected year. Earlier years remain open to corrections and new threads. Recognition rewards verified evidence and challenges. Annual views eventually reference one shared graph, enabling separately reviewed connections across years with explicit lag and competing causes. See `YEAR_EXPANSION.md` for readiness and cross-year review rules. The active contribution year remains 1816.
