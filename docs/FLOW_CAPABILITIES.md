# Distinct investigations, shared research tools

16 October 2026. Updated for 0.5.62. Implementation assessment, not a human usability study. The first story is the reference for depth, not a screen template to copy.

## What should be shared

All three stories can enter the same source inspector, focused thread, player-owned thesis/evidence board, research and optional AI return, rival test, revision history and version-specific portfolio/review. Saving, recovery, appearance, keyboard support and honest evidence boundaries belong everywhere. Existing points acknowledge activities; source inspection or a completed case does not verify a historical conclusion.

## Prioritized work

1. Functional validation of the Dutch separate-ledger activity and its exact source/research route is covered by relief-trace; human comprehension remains to test. Its main constraint is not implying that public imports and the decree's reserved rye were the same stock. Recipient evidence must stay missing until sourced.
2. Extend the Andes activity only where sources support its own problem: distinguish production capacity from delivery to particular columns, then separate logistics from battle explanation. The 0.5.62 source audit adds a prospective march/provisioning report in a later printed copy, not proof of execution. Seek later movement/issue/receipt evidence and authenticate transmission before showing routes, quantities or losses. Do not invent a transport simulation with historical-looking outcomes.
3. Pilot the three entry points with humans: do they recognize a different investigative task, form their own bounded explanation, know the next source to seek and want to return? Functional test completion is not a substitute.
4. Consider a short, own-words case notebook outside the full thesis if observed player behavior supports it. Reuse saved draft infrastructure; do not require all academic planning fields merely to express a first explanation.

These are design hypotheses and priorities, not evidence of increased engagement or retention. No new agent/persona playtest or human trial is claimed in this assessment.
