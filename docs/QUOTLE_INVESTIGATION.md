# Quote to history: an introductory research investigation

Candidate 0.5.89 · 4 November 2026. This is a learning-design hypothesis, not a demonstrated improvement in critical thinking.

## Purpose and route

One short, optional investigation introduces a repeatable process: question → research → check → review → revise. The learner then applies source-scope and reviewer-checking decisions inside the existing first harvest story. The story shelf offers the entry; `?practice=quotle` opens it directly. Returning learners can keep using the original stories and research workspace.

The example separates use of a phrase from its invention. The learner records an initial reaction, chooses an AI research request, inspects a clickable source trail, decides what a passage supports, assesses a proposed correction, and writes a bounded explanation and next source to seek. The review comparison is withheld until the learner supplies their own decision and reasoning. Explanatory feedback is guidance, not an evaluation of their free text.

All assistant and reviewer statements are constructed teaching examples, labeled wherever displayed. This does not execute an external AI service, capture a real assistant exchange, or establish how often any model makes these errors. The optional prompt includes the actual question, claim to test, source leads and output requirements so it makes sense outside the app.

## Transfer into the first story

After an explicit handoff, the harvest story retains its existing evidence, uncertainty and writing mechanics:

1. At the Czech evidence scene, the learner opens WT-1816-0006 in the existing source inspector, then distinguishes regional grain/price findings from a claim about every household. The opening is recorded as viewed, not independently verified.
2. Only after the learner reveals the Korean reports, a constructed review claim extends good rice reports to an absence of hunger. The learner chooses a response and explains the evidence boundary before opening comparison guidance. Administrative crop descriptions are not silently converted into measured yields or household food access.
3. The learner writes and revises their explanation in the existing harvest notebook, then follows its existing thesis/research/export route. No answer or evidence role is filled in for them by the practice.

The practice summary is expandable at the story ending. One download contains quote decisions, transfer notes, sources/locators, explicit limitations and the learner's harvest notebook. It is separate from historical source exports; the quote is not evidence for an 1816 thesis.

## State, access and progress

`worldthreads-quotle-practice-v1` holds only practice choices and writing. Local saves detect other-tab changes and preserve unsupported/corrupt saved originals. A download remains available if saving is unavailable or conflicted. The quote exercise does not write to the historical corpus, thesis, portfolio, harvest notebook or historical reward store. Handoff intentionally starts the first story at its opening while keeping existing progress and drafts. The tutorial query parameter is removed on handoff so a later reload uses the normal story entry rather than forcing the introduction again. Browser Back/Forward retains the practice stage; quote-stage reload resumes it.

All actions are buttons/fields/links with keyboard access. Dark/light controls and text scaling apply. Explanations fold away; motion is limited to source-panel reveals and disabled with reduced motion. The stage icons indicate process location, not competence. Completion means that decisions and writing were recorded, never a verified historical conclusion or mastery certificate. There are no quote-stage points or badges for clicking sources or agreeing with feedback.
