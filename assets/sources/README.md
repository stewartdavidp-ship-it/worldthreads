# Printed evidence images

`bertling-1908-p21.png` and `bertling-1908-p22.png` are unretouched renders of PDF pages 32–33 (printed pp. 21–22, documents 18–19) in H. Bertling, *Documentos históricos referentes al paso de Los Andes efectuado en 1817 por el general San Martín* (Concepción, 1908).

Source: Biblioteca Nacional de Chile / Memoria Chilena, MC0027393. [Catalog and reproduction statement](https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/602/w3-article-9227.html); [digitized PDF](https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/archivos2/pdfs/MC0027393.pdf#page=32). Catalogued as patrimonio cultural común and permitted for reproduction. Retrieved and visually inspected 17 October 2026.

These show a later printed edition, not authenticated original manuscripts. OCR located passages; the printed images were used to check counts and datelines. See SRC-056, WT-1816-0083 and docs/RESEARCH_AUDIT.md for support and limits. The full source PDF is not committed.
